# CMMC CCA Assessor Mindset Guide

A free, scenario-based study tool for CMMC Certified CMMC Assessor (CCA) exam preparation — built to teach assessor reasoning, not memorization.

## 🎯 Study Modes

### 📖 Controls Reference
All 110 CMMC Level 2 controls organized by 14 domains. Filter by study status, implementation type (Policy, Config, Software, Hardware, Mixed), and search across all controls. Progress tracked locally.

### ✏️ Quiz Mode
Multiple choice, flashcards, type match, and domain match. Configurable question count with smart wrong-answer boosting — questions you miss appear more frequently until you get them right consistently.

### ⚖️ Professional Conduct (36 questions)
CoPC principles (POCMI), conflict of interest scenarios, cooling-off periods, consulting prohibitions, role boundaries (CCP vs CCA vs CCI), and real-world ethics situations that test how assessors are expected to behave before, during, and after an engagement.

### ⚡ Watch Out (16 questions)
The rules, distinctions, and edge cases that trip up even experienced assessors — POA&M restrictions, partial scoring logic, asset categorization traps, evidence terminology, and regulatory gotchas.

### 🎯 Assessor Judgment (84 scenarios)
Real-world assessment situations requiring applied reasoning: evidence evaluation, adequate vs. sufficient calls, scoping decisions, SSP accuracy gaps, inheritance verification, artifact matching, partial implementation scoring, and the EXAMINE/INTERVIEW/TEST method selection.

### 📋 CAP Process (46 questions)
All four CAP phases, roles (Lead CCA, QA Individual, Affirming Official, Authorized Certifying Official), mandatory vs. optional documents, in-brief and out-brief requirements, POA&M closeout rules, and CONDITIONAL vs. FINAL certification paths.

### 🗺️ Scoping Decisions (46 questions)
Asset categorization under pressure — CUI Assets, Security Protection Assets (SPA), Contractor Risk Managed Assets (CRMA), Specialized Assets (IoT, OT, GFE), and Out of Scope. Includes real-world scenarios: BYOD, conference room displays, guest WiFi, dev/test environments, and shared infrastructure.

### 📚 Know the Language (12 questions)
Precision terminology that matters on the exam — adequate vs. sufficient, approved vs. authorized, Lead CCA vs. Lead Assessor, Affirming Official vs. Authorized Certifying Official, CUI vs. FCI, and regulatory reference distinctions.

### 🧠 Mindset Mode
Read-only worked examples that teach the reasoning process behind assessor decisions. Each card presents a real scenario, walks through the thinking step by step, gives the correct call, and calls out the common trap. Covers asset categorization, evidence evaluation, partial scoring, EXAMINE/INTERVIEW/TEST method selection, Specialized Asset edge cases, and more.

### 🔬 Deep Dive Reference
All 110 controls with expandable detail — Assessment Objectives, Key Takeaways, Assessment Tips, and Assessor Focus notes including specific EXAMINE/INTERVIEW/TEST guidance per practice.

### 💡 Exam Strategy & Tips
Thinking frameworks for the exam: how to handle NOT/EXCEPT questions, extreme language traps, scenario reading strategy, and assessor mindset principles.

### 📊 Stats
Performance tracking across all question modes with domain-weighted breakdown mirroring the actual CCA exam blueprint: D1 (15%), D2 (20%), D3 (25%), D4 (40%).
